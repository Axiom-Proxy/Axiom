Key Historical Eras

The Renaissance & Reformation

1300 – 1650 CE

A rebirth of art, science, and humanism swept across Europe. Explore the works of da Vinci, Michelangelo, and the cultural transformations that reshaped Western thought.

The Age of Revolutions

1750 – 1850 CE

The American, French, and Industrial Revolutions redefined governance, economics, and society. Understand how Enlightenment ideas fueled political upheaval worldwide.

The World Wars

1914 – 1945 CE

Two global conflicts reshaped borders, ideologies, and the international order. Analyze the causes, major battles, and lasting consequences of WWI and WWII.
