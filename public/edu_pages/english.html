Literary Masterpieces

Romeo and Juliet

William Shakespeare

Shakespeare's timeless tragedy of star-crossed lovers. Explore themes of love, fate, family conflict, and the power of language through one of the most-performed plays in history.

To Kill a Mockingbird

Harper Lee

A landmark American novel that tackles racial injustice and moral growth through the eyes of young Scout Finch. Essential reading for understanding empathy and courage.

The Great Gatsby

F. Scott Fitzgerald

A masterpiece of the Jazz Age. Fitzgerald's critique of the American Dream, wealth, and identity remains as relevant today as it was nearly a century ago.
