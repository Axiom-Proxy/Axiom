Core Economic Concepts

Supply & Demand

The foundational model of economics. Learn how prices emerge from the interaction of producers and consumers, and how shifts in supply or demand affect market equilibrium.

Opportunity Cost

Every choice involves a trade-off. Understand how economists measure the value of the next-best alternative foregone, and why this concept is central to rational decision-making.

Compound Interest

Einstein called it the eighth wonder of the world. Learn how interest on interest grows wealth exponentially over time, and why starting early matters more than starting big.
