Featured Artists

Leonardo da Vinci

1452 – 1519, Renaissance Italy

The quintessential Renaissance man — painter, inventor, and scientist. His works including the Mona Lisa and The Last Supper remain among the most recognizable and studied artworks in history.

Frida Kahlo

1907 – 1954, Mexico

Known for her vibrant self-portraits that explore identity, pain, and the human experience. Kahlo's work blends Mexican folk art with surrealism and remains profoundly influential today.

Vincent van Gogh

1853 – 1890, Post-Impressionism

His bold colors and expressive brushwork revolutionized modern art. Despite struggling during his lifetime, van Gogh created over 2,100 artworks that now define the post-impressionist movement.
