Axiom Recovery

Axiom lets you edit its own files, which means it lets you break them. This page is not served from your filesystem, so it still works when the desktop does not.

Start with Restore site files: it throws away your edits to Axiom itself and leaves everything in Home alone. Wipe disk is the bigger hammer — it deletes your files too. You can also open any page with ?__axiom_raw=1 to load the shipped version of it once.
